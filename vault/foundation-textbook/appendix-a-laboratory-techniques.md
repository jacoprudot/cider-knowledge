Appendix A – Key Laboratory Techniques


## A. the measurement of specific gravity in cider & perry (hydrometer)


METHOD

- Degas and filter the sample if required.

- Firstly rinse, then fill the cylinder with an appropriate amount of sample.

- Place the hydrometer in the sample with bulb facing down.

- Spin the hydrometer to ensure that it does not stick to the sides of the cylinder and all air bubbles are

dispersed from side of hydrometer.

- Wait for the hydrometer to settle and read the specific gravity by measuring the bottom of the liquid meniscus.

B. THE MEASUREMENT OF pH IN CIDER & PERRY (pH METER) METHOD

- Calibrate pH meter before each batch of samples using buffer solutions of pH 7.00 & 4.00.

- Degas the sample as required.

- Rinse and fill a beaker with an appropriate amount of sample (and add a magnetic follower (“Flea”).

- (Place the beaker onto a magnetic stirrer).

- Lower the probe into the liquid.

- Read the value indicated by the pH meter when stabilized.

- The results should be expressed to 2 decimal places.

- Rinse and gently blot dry the probe between samples. Electrodes should never be left dry for any period of

time. (Replace into pH 4.00 buffer).


## C. the measurement of total acidity of cider & perry (titration)


SAFETY Sodium Hydroxide Solution, 0.2 mol l-1 (0.2M / 0.2N) Wear eye protection Phenolphthalein Indicator Solution Wear eye protection METHOD

- Degas the sample as required.

- Rinse and fill the burette with 0.2 mol l-1 (0.2 M / 0.2N) sodium hydroxide solution. Note the reading of the

burette.

- Rinse out 250 ml conical flask with distilled water.

- Pipette 25 ml of the sample into a 250 ml conical flask and add 100 - 150 ml distilled water

- Add 6 drops of phenolphthalein indicator to the sample.

- Slowly run 0.2 mol l-1 (0.2M / 0.2N) sodium hydroxide from the burette into the sample; shaking the flask

often, until the whole sample has reached the pink end-point.

- Record the burette reading and calculate the amount of malic acid by using the following calculation:

Malic acid (gl-1) = Vol. of sodium hydroxide sol. used (ml) x 0.536. Report to 1 decimal place


## D. the measurement of free sulfur dioxide content of cider and perry


(Ripper Titration Method) SAFETY Sulfuric Acid Solution, 10% v/v - Wear eye protection Iodine Solution, 0.005 mol l-1 (0.005M / 0.01N) - Wear eye protection Iodine Indicator (Starch) - Wear eye protection METHOD 1. Rinse and fill the burette with 0.005 mol l-1 (0.005M / 0.01N) iodine solution. Note the reading of the burette. 2. Rinse out 250 ml conical flask with distilled water. 3. Pipette 25 ml of the sample into a 250 ml conical flask 4. Acidify by carefully adding 5 ml of 10% v/v sulfuric acid solution from an appropriate dispenser. 5. Add about 0.5 g iodine indicator (starch powder) or a few drops of starch solution to the sample and gently shake the contents of the conical flask until the indicator has dissolved fully. 6. Titrate using the 0.005 mol l-1 (0.005M / 0.01N)) iodine solution until a stable blue-grey color is obtained for approximately 10 seconds. 7. Record the burette reading and calculate the free sulfur dioxide content by using the following calculation: Free SO2 content (mg l-1) = Vol. of iodine sol. used (ml) x 12.8. Report to the nearest whole number.

THE MEASUREMENT OF TOTAL SULFUR DIOXIDE CONTENT OF CIDER AND PERRY (Ripper Titration Method) SAFETY Sulfuric Acid Solution, 10% v/v - Wear eye protection Sodium Hydroxide Solution (2.0M / 2.0N) – Wear eye protection Iodine Solution, 0.005 mol l-1 (0.005M / 0.01N) - Wear eye protection Iodine Indicator (Starch) - Wear eye protection METHOD 1. Rinse and fill the burette with 0.005 mol l-1 (0.005M / 0.01N) iodine solution. Note the reading of the burette. 2. Rinse out 250 ml conical flask with distilled water. 3. Pipette 25 ml of the sample into a 250 ml conical flask 4. Carefully add 5 ml of 2.0 mol l-1 (2.0M / 2.0N) sodium hydroxide solution from an appropriate dispenser, gently mix and allow to stand for exactly 5 minutes. 5. Acidify by carefully adding 5 ml of 10% v/v sulfuric acid solution from an appropriate dispenser. 6. Add about 0.5 g iodine indicator (starch powder) or a few drops of starch solution to the sample and gently shake the contents of the conical flask until the indicator has dissolved fully. 7. Titrate using the 0.005 mol l-1 (0.005M / 0.01N)) iodine solution until a stable blue-grey color is obtained for approximately 10 seconds. 8. Record the burette reading and calculate the free sulfur dioxide content by using the following calculation: Total SO2 content (mg l-1) = Vol. of iodine sol. used (ml) x 12.8. Report to the nearest whole number.
