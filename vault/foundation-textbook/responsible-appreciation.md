Cider and Perry - An Appreciation

The Cider Industry places a strong emphasis on the responsible appreciation of cider and perry by the consumer and all those involved in the manufacture, marketing, sales and promotion of the products concerned. In the USA, for example, the annual cost of alcohol abuse is estimated at $249 billion ($2.05 per Standard Drink); $191bn due to binge drinking; $179bn cost of lost productivity (Am. J. Prev. Med., 2016). 14.4 million Americans have alcoholism or related problems (NIAAA, 2020). The problem is worse for young people. For all those involved with the Cider Industry and the marketing, sales and promotion of its products, responsibility involves: Knowing and observing the laws associated with the sale of alcoholic beverages – notably prohibiting the sale of alcohol to young people (under the age of 21 in the USA); Fully recognizing – and taking action where and when appropriate (e.g. provision of clear consumer advice) - the dangers to human health, the problem of drink-driving and other social troubles (e.g. anti-social behavior) that can arise as a result of consuming alcohol; Have knowledge and understanding of (and apply as appropriate) the key facts associated with alcohol as summarised below. Most countries of the world have a simple and established way to communicate information about the alcohol content of drinks to the consumer. For example, “UK Units” are used in Britain. In the USA and Canada, the term “Standard Drink” is used, which are defined as follows: USA – Standard Drinks

- Definition:

— 1 “Standard Drink” = a drink that contains about 0.6 fluid ounces or 14 grams of “pure” alcohol Canada – Standard Drinks

- Definition:

— 1 “Standard Drink” = a drink that contains 13.6 grams of “pure” alcohol Relationship to Typical Servings:

- 355ml (12 ounce) can of 5% ABV beer

- 146ml (5 ounce) glass of 10 – 12% ABV wine

- 44ml (1.5 ounce) of 40% ABV spirit

Recommended Limits in North America (2015-2020 U.S. Dietary Guidelines for Americans):

- Men: No more than 2 Standard Drinks per day

- Women: No more than 1 Standard Drink per day


## Alcohol & the Body


Alcohol is absorbed into the blood stream very quickly after consumption and results in the Blood Alcohol Concentration (BAC), which is measured in milligrams (mg) per ml blood. A BAC of 50 - 80 mg alcohol in 100 ml blood (0.5 - 0.8 mgml-1) is typical the level at and above which it is an offence to drive (it varies between different countries). After drinking, the amount of alcohol which gets into the blood affected by:

- Quantity consumed — Size of person

- Gender — Food eaten

## Opportunities: 1. The Heritage of Cider & Perry


One of the many opportunities that can be exploited is to promote the great and long-established heritage of the Cider Industry across the world:

- Defines regional cultures – West of England, Asturias (Spain), Normandy (France), The American

- A very versatile drink

## Opportunities: 3. Cider & Perry with Food


Food-pairing and use in cooking, as well as providing another wonderful opportunity, is also a great way to promote the responsible consumption of cider & perry. The following points should be considered:

- Size & weight and flavour intensity between cider/perry and food

- Complement or contrast

- Be careful with bitter ciders and salty foods

- Astringent products can match well with protein-rich foods

- For spicy foods – match with refreshing styles of cider. The more spice, the sweeter the cider
