Cider and Perry – Table of Attributes The following “Table of Attributes” (Table 6) has been designed as a tool to assist in the sensory analysis and appreciation of samples of cider and perry. Similar to flavour wheels also used in the cider, wine and beer industries and other food and drink sectors, it has four key purposes: 1. Provides a structured approach and categorization to describe cider and perry in objective and non-judgmental terms; 2. Provides a lexicon, common vocabulary and agreed terminology for describing and profiling the products concerned; 3. Acts as an aide memoire when undertaking organoleptic assessment; 4. Designed to help consumers and customers gain a real appreciation of the wide and diverse range of products available – and can be used in marketing, sales and promotional activities. The following guidance notes are provided to assist in the use of the Table of Attributes: q The table is structured in the order that organoleptic assessment is to be undertaken; q The main organoleptic Categories organized into a number of Groups; q Each organoleptic Group is provided with its own Descriptors – these aim to standardise the language or terminology to be used (i.e. an agreed ‘word-to-attribute’ correlation); q An explanation and range of Synonyms, often used by assessors, for each Descriptor are also provided. An example of a Product Profile Form – used when assessing the products concerned - is included in this booklet after the table. Cider & Cheese - A Perfect Match!
